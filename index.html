A ban is a decision with no appeal. This gives the appeal a verdict.

A platform funds a compensation pool. A banned user files an appeal bound to the platform, the content URL and the stated ban reason, with a stake behind it. An on-chain jury fetches the content and rules whether the reason actually matches it: a wrong ban pays compensation from the pool and returns the stake, a justified ban forfeits the stake into the pool so hopeless appeals cost money. One appeal per decision, so the same ban cannot be farmed twice.

How it works

  1. fund_compensation_pool (payable) — the platform puts GEN in the pool that pays for wrong bans.
  2. file_appeal (payable) — the appeal is bound to a platform, a content URL and the stated ban reason, and locked to a decision digest. Stake 0.01–10 GEN, compensation 0.001–100 GEN and never more than 100x the stake. The same decision can only be appealed once.
  3. judge_appeal — the jury fetches the content on-chain and rules whether the stated reason actually matches it. An overturn is booked as committed, which the platform can no longer withdraw; if the pool cannot cover it the appeal becomes UNFUNDED.
  4. settle_appeal — exactly once: an overturned ban pays the compensation and returns the stake; a justified ban forfeits the stake into the pool.
  5. activate_unfunded — after a top-up, an UNFUNDED overturn can be promoted to payable by anyone.
  6. reclaim_stake — the appellant always gets out: on demand when the pool could not pay, or once an appeal has sat unjudged past the 7-day window. Reclaiming voids the claim.
  7. withdraw_pool — the platform takes forfeited stakes and only the uncommitted pool. Compensation already owed cannot be withdrawn.
